01 About the Project

Practical experience in examining public institutions

Youth Shadow Cabinet Uzbekistan is a student-led civic engagement initiative that gives young people practical experience in understanding, researching, and examining the work of government institutions. Each participant is assigned to a government ministry and monitors its recent projects, reforms, investments, programs, achievements, and other public developments. Every month, participants select one significant development, investigate it using reliable sources, review its results, and prepare a short Policy Letter with practical recommendations.

Youth Shadow Cabinet Uzbekistan is not a political party, an opposition organization, or a platform created only to criticize the government. A participant may conclude that a government initiative is successful, that it needs improvement, or that its results are mixed — all of these conclusions are valid if they are supported by reliable evidence.

02 Mission & Core Values

Evidence first, always

To give young people in Uzbekistan practical, evidence-based experience in understanding government institutions and public policy, and a professional, constructive way to participate in civic life.

01

Evidence-Based

Every claim in a Policy Letter is supported by verifiable, reliable sources — not opinion or assumption.

02

Non-Partisan

The project takes no political position of its own; each letter's conclusion follows only from the evidence.

03

Constructive

The goal is not criticism for its own sake, but practical, realistic recommendations.

04

Professional

All communication — with ministries, the public, and each other — is respectful and measured.

05

Transparent

Published letters, review standards, and the project's purpose are all publicly stated, not hidden.

07 Apply / Join

Research real policy. Publish real work.

Apply to join Youth Shadow Cabinet Uzbekistan and be assigned a ministry that matches your interests.

  • 01Term length: 3 months, with the option to continue based on project needs.
  • 02One Policy Letter per month, typically 3–4 hours of work.
  • 03All positions are voluntary — no salary or compensation.
  • 04You can request full name credit or lighter visibility at any time.
  • 05Respectful, professional representation of the project is expected in all communication.
Applicants under 18 will be asked for guardian awareness as part of the application. No personal contact details are ever published — only ministry, term, and, optionally, name and photo.

Who can apply?

Students aged 15 and older with an interest in government policy, research, and civic engagement. No prior experience required — just roughly 3–4 hours a month and a genuine interest in the field connected to the ministry you choose.
